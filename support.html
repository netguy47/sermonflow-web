Support

Everything you need to get help with Sermon Flow, and the small print around subscriptions and refunds, on one page.

How to get help

Sermon Flow is a small app made by one person. If something is not working, or something is not working the way you expected, please write and you will get a reply from a person, not a ticket robot.

  1. Open the app and try the thing again — most problems are a stale sync. Sermon Flow stores your notes in the cloud, so a note that looks missing usually appears after the app is reopened with a connection.
  2. If you are on a paid subscription and a Pro feature is not unlocked, use Settings → Restore Purchases (also on the paywall). This asks Apple to re-deliver your receipts and is the fix for a purchase that has not carried over to a new device.
  3. If it still is not right, email us with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. If it involves a note, email from the same device the note was created on, so we can find it.

Your subscription, cancellation and refunds

Sermon Flow Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store, monthly or annual. The purchase is made between you and Apple, and Apple handles the payment, the billing, and the refunds.

  • Cancel: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sermon Flow Pro → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for; you keep Pro until then.
  • Refund: the fastest route is Apple itself — support.apple.com → "Refunds and Subscriptions", or the "Report a Problem" link in your App Store purchase history. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases except where the law requires it. Please tell us as well, so we can act on the outcome.
  • Restore: if you have paid and Pro has not unlocked, tap Restore Purchases. Do this before contacting support — it solves the problem immediately in most cases.
  • Price changes: if a price changes, Apple notifies you before the renewal, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

Common questions

Where are my notes?

In the cloud, attached to an anonymous account created on your device. There is no email address and no password behind it. If you uninstall the app, we may have no way to associate those notes with you — so export anything you want to keep first. See the Privacy Policy.

Is my text sent to an AI provider?

Only the passage, topic and instructions you type into the Create tab, and only when you tap Generate Outline and confirm the disclosure sheet. Your notes are never sent to an AI provider. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Does the app work offline?

Reading and writing on the device works offline. The bundled World English Bible works offline. Notes save locally and sync to the cloud when the connection returns; on a device that has never synced, the timeline will be empty until it does.

Where does the scripture text come from?

The World English Bible, a public-domain translation, bundled with the app. The app links to worldenglish.bible from Settings → Scripture Credits.

Privacy requests

You can delete any note yourself, at any time, from inside the app — open the note and choose Delete Note, or swipe it in the timeline. If you want your remaining notes deleted from cloud storage in bulk, email the privacy address above from the device the notes were created on, with the anonymous account identifier, and we will delete them. We do not sell, rent or share your notes, and we do not use them for tracking or advertising.
