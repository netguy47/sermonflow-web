Sermon Flow

Scripture notes for preachers — write, organise and search your sermon notes, with the World English Bible bundled and an optional AI-assisted outline drafter.

The pages that matter

  • Support — how to get help, contact routes, and what to do about subscriptions, cancellation and refunds.
  • Privacy Policy — what the app stores, where it stores it, what is never collected, and how to have it deleted.
  • Terms of Service — the terms you agree to when you use the app, including the subscription terms.

In short

  • No account, no email address, no password. Notes are attached to an anonymous account created on your device.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no ad identifier.
  • Your notes are yours. You can delete any note yourself, at any time.
  • The AI drafting feature is optional, off until you ask for it, and the app tells you which provider handles your request before it sends anything.
