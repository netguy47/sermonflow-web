3. The Service

Sermon Flow is a private writing and reference tool for people who prepare sermons. It lets you:

  • write and organise sermon notes, and search your own notes;
  • have scripture references detected in your text and see the passage text from a Bible translation bundled in the App;
  • flag a note as a "Life Application";
  • view your most recent note, or a scripture of the day, on a Home Screen widget; and

The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no content feed. It does not publish or share your notes with anyone.

3.1 Scripture text

Scripture quotations shown by the App are from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation, which is credited in the App under Settings → Scripture Credits. Scripture references are detected by a text-matching engine and scored for confidence; the App may occasionally detect something that is not a scripture reference, or miss a reference, particularly where the text uses an unusual abbreviation or a non-English book name. Always check a detected reference against your own text before relying on it.

3.2 The "Create" tab and the Sermon Architect screen

The Create tab can request a sermon outline draft from our own drafting service, which runs on Google Cloud and calls Google Gemini on our behalf. The provider's access credential is held on our server and is never contained in the App distributed through the App Store.

When you use this feature, the only information transmitted is the passage, topic and instructions you type into that screen. Your sermon notes, your account details and other content are not sent with that request, and the drafting service rejects requests containing additional fields.

The output is a draft produced by a machine, not Scripture and not a quotation. It is labelled in the App as "AI-generated draft—review Scripture references and theological claims." It may be inaccurate, and it may contain a Scripture reference or theological claim that is wrong. You are responsible for checking every reference against the Bible and your own sources before you preach it. Nothing in the App or the draft should be treated as a substitute for your own preparation.

The feature is optional, and the rest of the App — writing notes, saving, searching, organising, presenting, reminders and the widget — works whether or not you use it and whether or not the drafting service is available. If the drafting service is unavailable or unconfigured, the App says so plainly and produces no outline; it never substitutes a sample or invented draft.
